# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Indian mobile-first shoppers (buyers) using Any&All — a live-shopping / live-auction marketplace. They browse on phones, often on 4G, one-handed, in short sessions. Core jobs: discover live shows, watch + chat, bid in fast auctions, buy pinned products, pay instantly (UPI-first), track orders, and message sellers. Sellers are served by the website's Seller Hub; the app is the buyer surface.

## Product Purpose
The Any&All Android/iOS app is the primary buyer surface for live commerce: find something live *right now*, get into the room fast, and transact with zero friction. Success = time-to-live-room measured in seconds, bids/purchases completed without leaving the moment, and repeat daily visits driven by live/scheduled show discovery.

## Brand Personality
Premium, clean, trustworthy. Dark navy + electric blue identity shared with anynall.com (serif display type for brand moments, Inter for UI, mono for numbers/timers). Energy comes from the LIVE red accent and real content (thumbnails, prices, countdowns) — not decoration.

## Anti-references
- The anynall.com website layout itself: no landing-page hero structure, no stacked marketing sections, no web-style headers inside the app.
- Generic template/e-commerce starter UI (identical card grids, icon+heading+text repetition).
- Shrunk-website feel: tiny link rows, centered hero copy, footer-like blocks.

## Design Principles
1. **Live first** — whatever is live right now is the most important content in the app; it leads every surface.
2. **Screens, not pages** — each screen has one job and one primary action; depth over stacking.
3. **Thumb-reachable commerce** — bid/buy/pay actions live in the bottom half of the screen; timers and prices are glanceable.
4. **Real content is the decoration** — thumbnails, prices, countdowns and sellers carry the visual energy; chrome stays quiet.
5. **Trust at the moment of money** — clear totals, saved address/method, verified-server pricing, no surprises in checkout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Dark-only theme must keep body text ≥4.5:1 on navy surfaces. Touch targets ≥44dp. Respect reduced-motion (no essential info conveyed only by animation). Copy stays short and bilingual-friendly (en-IN audience).
